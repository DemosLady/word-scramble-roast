Tips to Unscramble Letters Faster

Look for Common Patterns

English words follow predictable patterns. Look for letter combos like TH, SH, CH, QU, and ING. Spotting these clusters inside the scrambled letters gives you a head start on cracking the word.

Vowels First

Separate the vowels (A, E, I, O, U) from consonants. Most English words alternate between consonants and vowels, so arranging them in pairs can reveal the answer quickly.

Prefixes & Suffixes

Check if common prefixes (UN-, RE-, PRE-, DIS-) or suffixes (-LY, -ED, -ING, -TION, -NESS) are hiding in the letters. Locking those down narrows the possibilities dramatically.
