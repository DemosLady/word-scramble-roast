About Word Scramble Roast Edition

Word Scramble Roast Edition is a free, timed word puzzle game. You are given a set of scrambled letters and have to unscramble the original word before the clock runs out. When the round ends, an AI delivers a playful roast based on how you did — the kind of thing you screenshot and send to a friend who thinks they are smarter than you.

What Makes It Different

Most word games are either slow and turn-based or limited to one puzzle a day. This one is built for quick, repeatable rounds: pick a difficulty, play as much as you want, and watch your unscrambling speed improve over time. There is no signup, no paywall, and no limit on how many times you can play.

  • Three difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, and Hard.
  • A timer that keeps every round tense.
  • One hint per word for when you are truly stuck.
  • A shareable AI roast at the end of each game.
